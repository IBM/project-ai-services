# Annual Report 2024

## Executive Summary

Revenue grew by fifteen percent year-on-year driven by strong performance in the European market.

## Financial Results

| Metric | 2023 | 2024 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | €10M | €11.5M | +15% |
| EBITDA | €2.1M | €2.6M | +24% |
| Headcount | 142 | 158 | +11% |

## Operational Review

The operations team successfully reduced average delivery time from five days to three days. Warehouse automation introduced in Q2 contributed to a twenty percent reduction in fulfilment errors. Customer satisfaction scores improved to an all-time high of 4.7 out of 5.0 based on the annual survey conducted in December across all major customer segments in Europe and North America.

## Outlook

The company expects continued growth in 2025, with a target revenue increase of ten percent.

## Priorities

- Expand the warehouse network in Europe.
- Reduce delivery time further.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
